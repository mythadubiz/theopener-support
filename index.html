Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings (gear icon) → scroll to "Restore Purchases" and tap. Your premium access will be restored instantly.

How do I change the language?
Open Settings → Language → pick from 17 languages. All 1440 questions are translated, no internet needed.

How do I enable or disable categories?
Open Settings → scroll to Categories → toggle each of the 10 categories on or off.

What is included in Premium?
All 10 categories (1440 questions total), all 17 languages, all 50 card designs. One-time purchase, yours forever.

Is my data collected?
No personal data, ever — no name, no email, no account, and nothing you write in the app. Your cards and settings stay on your device. Since version 1.14 the app can also record anonymous usage statistics to help us improve it, but only if you agree when asked at first launch; you can change your mind any time in Settings.
